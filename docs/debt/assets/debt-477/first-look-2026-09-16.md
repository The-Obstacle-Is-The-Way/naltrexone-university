# Independent first look — 2026-09-16

Written after viewing the production 1440 px full-page captures and before reading either DEBT-477 or DEBT-478. Dark scheme, DPR 1, Chromium; viewport height 1000 px.

## Home

The hero's filled Get Started pill is visibly shorter than the adjacent outline View pricing pill. Capitalization alternates between Get Started and View pricing/Sign in. The Features header is left aligned while the hero, pricing heading, and final CTA header are centered. The last Get Started control has an arrow and an outline/metallic treatment unlike the earlier filled primary action. The footer presents product, account, and legal links in one undifferentiated line. “100% Mobile Responsive” feels like a technical implementation claim rather than a study outcome. The copy claims dashboard streaks and trends; this needs a source check before treating it as accurate.

## Pricing

The plans span almost the full content width; repeated dense disclosure paragraphs dominate the comparison. Monthly and annual CTA baselines differ. Terms and Privacy links repeat in both disclosure boxes and the footer. Back to Home duplicates the brand navigation. Card text is readable and left aligned; the screenshot does not establish a reason to center it. The signed-out page advertises a trial before the visitor's past subscription history is known; eligibility must be evaluated after authentication. These are observations, not yet findings or legal conclusions.
