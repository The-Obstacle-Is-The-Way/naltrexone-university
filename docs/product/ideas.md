# Product Ideas

Unscheduled ideas: worth keeping, not yet decided or planned. Each line gives the idea and the closed record or brainstorm that holds its thinking, which [the ledger](../records/closed.md) resolves. When the owner picks one, it is specified in a product document under `docs/product/`, as [the product map](./README.md) describes; a record is only for a defect, debt or an investigation it uncovers.

- **Softer dark-mode borders** for read-only containers and subordinate separators, where the uniform bright border still reads as heavy (BS-044).
- **A bookmark icon toggle**, filled or unfilled, in place of the text pills for adding and removing a bookmark (BS-052).
- **Regroup the question-review action bar**, so the bookmark no longer sits between the reattempt and navigation controls (BS-059).
- **Practice and feedback enhancements:** a clinical pearl as a seed field, reference-section styling, a running score, collapsing the card after submit, difficulty and topic tags, and a direct-URL context mismatch (DEBT-337).
- **Structured data for the public pages:** `Organization`, and a `Product` and `Offer` derived from the real price source. First prove that missing JSON-LD fails, and that a changed Offer amount fails the displayed-price test (DEBT-479 step 4).
- **Practice Starter question-count polish:** four open decisions on how the starter presents the count when filters match fewer questions than asked; the core fix, an availability message before Start, shipped (BS-014).
- **Bookmarks in History:** a bookmark-status filter and a bookmark marker on the Questions tab's rows, as Quick Practice's status filter already offers; today a bookmark is one click away on the review page (from the bookmarks page dossier, deleted by DEBT-518).
- **Content feedback and vocabulary follow-ups:** question feedback without its attempt or session cannot identify the revision it reports, and vocabulary definitions are repeated across files. Neither is a confirmed defect (DEBT-531).
