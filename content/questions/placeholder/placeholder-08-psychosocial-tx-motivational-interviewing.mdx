## Stem

Which statement best characterizes motivational interviewing in substance use treatment?

## Explanation

Motivational interviewing is a collaborative, goal-oriented style of communication that strengthens personal motivation and commitment to change by eliciting and exploring the person’s reasons for change within an atmosphere of acceptance and compassion.
